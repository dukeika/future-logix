# Future Logix ₦40,000/month acquisition plan

Prepared 16 September 2026. Companion to `schoolsrep-launch.md` (the free organic plan).
This document adds the paid layer, the lead-handling routine, and the weekly review rules.

## The decision in one paragraph

Spend the whole ₦40,000 on one offer, one channel, one audience: Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
click-to-WhatsApp ads sending Nigerian private-school decision makers into a WhatsApp
conversation about the SchoolsRep guided trial. Services (AI automation, web apps, AWS,
modernization) start at ₦2.5m–₦5m and are sold through founder conversations, so they run on
the free channels (LinkedIn, referrals, direct outreach), not on this budget. Splitting ₦40k
across two audiences would leave neither with enough data to learn from.

Why SchoolsRep first:
- It already has a campaign page (`/schoolsrep/free-trial`), a trial funnel on schoolsrep.com,
  drafted posts, WhatsApp scripts, and seven ready-made SchoolsRep image tiles.
- September is the start of first term. Fee collection and parent calls are at their most
  painful right now, so the message lands.
- School owners message, they do not fill forms. Click-to-WhatsApp matches how they buy.

Assumptions: ₦40,000 is roughly US$25–27 at current rates, so this is a learning budget, not
a scale budget. Meta enforces a minimum daily budget of about ₦1,000 for this objective.
No tools are needed: Meta Business Suite, WhatsApp Business, Canva free, and the admin leads
desk are all free.

Do not spend on: Google Ads (₦40k buys too few high-intent clicks), boosting from the
Instagram app (worse targeting than Ads Manager), influencers, or directories.

## Handling leads properly (the part that decides whether ₦40k pays back)

Every lead lands in one of two places and both end up in the admin leads desk
(`https://futurelogix.ng/admin/leads`):

- Website form enquiries arrive automatically, with the campaign, ad, landing page, and
  referrer attached.
- WhatsApp, phone, referral, and social leads are logged by hand with "Log a lead" inside
  the first hour. Put the ad's `utm_content` name in the "Campaign / ad" field.

Daily routine (20 minutes, twice a day, 9am and 5pm):
1. Open the leads desk. Work "Follow-ups due" first, then "New".
2. Reply on WhatsApp within 15 minutes during 8am–8pm. Speed is the biggest conversion lever
   for Nigerian buyers.
3. After every conversation, update the status and write one line in Notes: school size,
   current tools, main pain, what was agreed, objection if any.
4. Set the next follow-up date before you close the row. Nothing sits without a date.

Pipeline meaning:
- New: nobody has replied yet.
- Contacted: we replied, waiting on them.
- Qualified: real school, real decision maker, named pain.
- Proposal sent: trial link, pricing, or walkthrough date shared.
- Won: trial started or invoice paid. Create the invoice from the invoice desk.
- Lost: said no or went quiet after the day-14 follow-up. Record the reason in Notes.
- Resolved: not a sales lead (support, spam, test records).

Follow-up cadence for anyone who goes quiet: day 1, day 3, day 7, day 14, then mark Lost.
Use the "Follow-Up" script from `schoolsrep-launch.md`.

First-week housekeeping: mark the eight "QA" and "Local QA" test records from March as
Resolved so the Open view only shows real people.

## Weekly review (Friday, 30 minutes)

Record these numbers in a simple sheet each week:

| Metric | Where to read it |
|---|---|
| Spend | Ads Manager |
| Conversations started | Ads Manager (Messaging conversations started) |
| Cost per conversation | Spend ÷ conversations |
| Leads logged | Admin leads desk, filter by campaign |
| Qualified | Leads desk, status Qualified or later |
| Guided demos / trials started | Leads desk, status Proposal sent or Won |
| Best ad by cost per conversation | Ads Manager, ad level |

Decision rules:
- After ₦7,000 spent: if cost per conversation is above ₦800, pause the weakest ad and
  replace its creative. If any ad is under ₦300, it gets the boost budget that week.
- After two weeks: if fewer than 5 qualified leads, the problem is targeting or the greeting
  question, not the budget. Tighten the audience to Lagos and Abuja only and change the hook.
- After four weeks: if at least one trial started, keep the same plan for month two and move
  the reserve into the daily budget. If none, switch the offer in month two to a 20-minute
  "school operations check-up" call before asking for a trial.

Targets for month one (assumptions to validate, not promises):
40–100 WhatsApp conversations, 10–20 qualified schools, 3–6 guided demos, 1–3 trials started.

## Month two and beyond (not now)

- Add a Meta Pixel behind the existing cookie consent so ads can optimise on
  "WhatsApp click" and "form submitted" site events.
- Build a lookalike audience from the qualified leads once there are 50+.
- Publish one proof story from the first trial school; proof lowers cost per conversation
  more than any targeting change.
- Only then consider a second offer (AI automation for SMEs) with its own budget.
