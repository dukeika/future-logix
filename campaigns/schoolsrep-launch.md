# SchoolsRep Campaign Launch Plan

## Campaign Positioning

Future Logix is the main trust and acquisition website. SchoolsRep is the flagship product being marketed through Future Logix, with the buyer moved into a guided demo, WhatsApp conversation, or product trial only after the core trust questions are answered.

Primary landing page:
`https://futurelogix.ng/schoolsrep/free-trial`

Product trial destination:
`https://schoolsrep.com/trial?utm_source=futurelogix&utm_medium=campaign_page&utm_campaign=schoolsrep_launch&utm_content=guided_trial`

Core offer:
Start a guided SchoolsRep trial before committing to a paid term.

Primary audience:
Nigerian private school owners, directors, administrators, bursars, heads of academics, and operations leads in schools with roughly 100-1,500 students.

## Campaign Message

SchoolsRep helps Nigerian private schools bring attendance, fees, results, parent communication, and admin records into one simpler workflow. Future Logix gives schools a guided way to evaluate fit, migration, support, pricing, and data protection before they pay for rollout.

## Buyer Pain Angles

- We currently use Excel, notebooks, and WhatsApp for school records.
- Parents keep calling for balances, updates, or results.
- Fee follow-up is difficult to track during the term.
- Result processing is stressful at the end of the term.
- School owners want clarity before paying a full-term software fee.

## Two-Week Launch Cadence

Day 1:
Publish the Future Logix campaign page and share the first founder/company post.

Day 2:
Send direct WhatsApp outreach to known school owners, administrators, and referral contacts.

Day 3:
Publish a short post focused on fee tracking and parent reminders.

Day 4:
Follow up with everyone who clicked, replied, or asked for details.

Day 5:
Publish a post focused on Excel-to-system migration.

Day 6:
Review inbound questions and turn repeated objections into FAQ copy.

Day 7:
Send a concise weekly recap post with the guided trial link.

Day 8:
Run another direct outreach batch by role: owners, bursars, admins, academic heads.

Day 9:
Publish a post focused on school owners approving software with less risk.

Day 10:
Follow up with demo prospects and move qualified schools into trial or scheduled walkthrough.

Day 11:
Publish a comparison post: scattered tools versus one school operations workflow.

Day 12:
Ask early trial users for objections, missing clarity, and testimonial permission.

Day 13:
Publish a data protection and trust post.

Day 14:
Review metrics and decide whether to scale outreach, refine the landing copy, or launch a paid ad test.

### Post 3: Migration

A common concern from schools is simple:

"Our records are already in Excel. Can we move without disrupting the term?"

That is exactly why the SchoolsRep campaign starts from a guided trial.

Before a school commits, the conversation should cover:

- student records
- parent contacts
- class structure
- staff access
- fees and balances
- results and attendance workflow
- support during rollout

Future Logix helps schools review those questions before they choose a paid plan.

https://futurelogix.ng/schoolsrep/free-trial

School software is not just a feature decision. It is a trust decision.

Before a school pays for a platform, leadership needs clear answers on:

- who can access records
- how parents are included
- what happens during migration
- how payments and manual transfers are reviewed
- what support exists after setup
- how pricing fits the school size
